Our Story

Born From a Love
of Real Burgers

Burger Byte started as a humble weekend pop-up in Kuala Lumpur, built on one belief — a great burger deserves great ingredients. We source our beef fresh daily, bake our buns in-house, and hand-craft every sauce from scratch.

No shortcuts, no freezers, no compromises. Today we serve hundreds of happy customers daily, but the heart of Burger Byte remains the same: honest food, made with care.
